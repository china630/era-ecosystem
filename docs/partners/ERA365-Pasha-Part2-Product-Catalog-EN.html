Why ERA 365

Built for Azerbaijan — not adapted later

Q · Why ERA 365?

A · Local legislation & operating reality first

Finance Core (and the stack around it) is designed around Azerbaijan rules: VÖEN, AZN, FIN, NAS-oriented accounting, local tax/trade practice, Asia/Baku dates — so Holding clients get a system that fits from day one.

What this means in practice
  • Legal entity & tax identity modeled for VÖEN orgs
  • Citizen FIN + foreigner passport paths in MDM
  • Finance tax / trade modules oriented to AZ compliance
  • Data Hub: CBAR FX, VÖEN company directory, local banks
  • Industry satellites post into Finance — one book for AZ auditors
